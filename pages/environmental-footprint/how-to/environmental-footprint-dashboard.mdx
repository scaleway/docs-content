The Scaleway Environmental Footprint dashboard helps you monitor and analyze the environmental impact of your cloud resource usage. It provides clear visibility into carbon and water consumption, enabling accurate reporting and informed optimization decisions.

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing actions in the intended Organization
- Environmental data is available only for clients using [supported Scaleway products](/environmental-footprint/additional-content/environmental-footprint-integration/). Data is generated daily and becomes visible the day after product activation. 

## Access the dashboards

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Cost & Impact Management** > **Environmental Footprint**. The **Overview** of your Organization environmental footprint appears, for the previous and current month.
2. By default, the chart shows the carbon consumption by product category. Click **Water** to switch to water consumption.

   <Lightbox image={image} alt="Overview of an Organization environmental footprint." />

3. Click **View details** to open the detailed dashboard. You can also access the dashboard by clicking the **Footprint** tab. 

   <Lightbox image={image2} alt="Carbon consumption for an Organization." />

## Filter consumption data

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Cost & Impact Management** > **Environmental Footprint**. The **Overview** of your Organization environmental footprint appears, for the previous and current month.
2. Click the **Footprint** tab to access the detailed dashboard.
3. In the filters section, click **All filters**. The **Filters** panel opens with all the available filters.
4. Select the filters among the following:
   * **Period** – Filters data by month using the following granularity:
      * Daily when selecting a single month.
      * Monthly when selecting multiple months.
   * **Service Category** – Specifies the type of service such as Compute or Storage. Selecting a service category filters the list of product categories, though you can select a product category directly.
   * **Product Category** – Specifies the product type. Values represent the total consumption of all matching products.
   * **Project** – Specifies Organization Projects to include. Leave empty to include all Projects. Deleted Projects appear as **Deleted**.
   * **Locality** – Specifies Regions or Zone where resources are deployed. Leave empty to include all regions.
5. Click **See results**. The **Filters** panel closes and the filtered Dashboard displays. Chart values represent total carbon or water consumption for the selected period. 
6. Click **Clear All** to reset your filters.

## View detailed consumption

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Cost & Impact Management** > **Environmental Footprint**. The **Overview** of your Organization environmental footprint appears, for the previous and current month.
2. Scroll down after the chart to view the detailed consumption per-product. Filters also apply to this list.

   <Lightbox image={image3} alt="Detailed environmental footprint per product." />
