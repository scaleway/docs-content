  ## Serverless Containers

  ### Serverless Containers supported endpoints

    The following [Serverless Containers](/serverless/containers/) endpoints are supported and will be logged in Audit Trail:

    - CreateContainer (`POST /containers/v1/regions/{region}/containers`)
    - UpdateContainer (`PATCH /containers/v1/regions/{region}/containers/{container_id}`)
    - DeleteContainer (`DELETE /containers/v1/regions/{region}/containers/{container_id}`)
    - RedeployContainer (`POST /containers/v1/regions/{region}/containers/{container_id}/redeploy`)
    - CreateDomain (`POST /containers/v1/regions/{region}/domains`)
    - UpdateDomain (`PATCH /containers/v1/regions/{region}/domains/{domain_id}`)
    - DeleteDomain (`DELETE /containers/v1/regions/{region}/domains/{domain_id}`)
    - CreateNamespace (`POST /containers/v1/regions/{region}/namespaces`)
    - UpdateNamespace (`PATCH /containers/v1/regions/{region}/namespaces/{namespace_id}`)
    - DeleteNamespace (`DELETE /containers/v1/regions/{region}/namespaces/{namespace_id}`)
    - CreateTrigger (`POST /containers/v1/regions/{region}/triggers`)
    - UpdateTrigger (`PATCH /containers/v1/regions/{region}/triggers/{trigger_id}`)
    - DeleteTrigger (`DELETE /containers/v1/regions/{region}/triggers/{trigger_id}`)
</Accordion>
