  ## Serverless Functions

  ### Serverless Functions supported endpoints

    The following [Serverless Functions](/serverless/functions/) endpoints are supported and will be logged in Audit Trail:

    - CreateCron (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/crons`)
    - UpdateCron (`PATCH /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/crons/{cron_id}`)
    - DeleteCron (`DELETE /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/crons/{cron_id}`)
    - CreateDomain (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/domains`)
    - DeleteDomain (`DELETE /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/domains/{domain_id}`)
    - CreateFunction (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/functions`)
    - UpdateFunction (`PATCH /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/functions/{function_id}`)
    - DeleteFunction (`DELETE /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/functions/{function_id}`)
    - DeployFunction (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/functions/{function_id}/deploy`)
    - CreateNamespace (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/namespaces`)
    - UpdateNamespace (`PATCH /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/namespaces/{namespace_id}`)
    - DeleteNamespace (`DELETE /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/namespaces/{namespace_id}`)
    - CreateToken (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/tokens`)
    - DeleteToken (`DELETE /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/tokens/{token_id}`)
    - CreateTrigger (`POST /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/triggers`)
    - UpdateTrigger (`PATCH /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/triggers/{trigger_id}`)
    - DeleteTrigger (`DELETE /functions/v1beta1/regions/{region}/triggers/{trigger_id}`)
</Accordion>
