**IP** **A**ddress **M**anager (IPAM) is Scaleway's tool for planning, tracking and managing the IP address space of Scaleway products. It acts as a single source of truth for the IP addresses of Scaleway resources.

[Read more about IPAM and future planned functionality](/ipam/concepts/#ipam).

This document shows you how to list and filter your IP addresses in the Scaleway console, whether they are [private IP addresses](/ipam/concepts/#private-ip-address) on your Private Networks, or public IP addresses exposing your resources to the internet.

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged into the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- At least one [reserved IP address](/ipam/how-to/reserve-ip/), or at least one resource [attached to a Private Network](/vpc/how-to/attach-resources-to-pn/)

## List and filter your IP addresses

1. Click **IPAM** in the **Network** section of the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu.

2. Select the region whose IP addresses you want to list, using the drop-down menu at the top of the page.

3. Select the **source** of the addresses to display:

    - **Private IPs**: private addresses on your Private Networks. Select a **VPC**, and optionally a **Private Network**, to display them.
    - **Public zonal IPs**: public addresses scoped to a single Availability Zone. Select a **zone** to display them.
    - **Public regional IPs**: public addresses scoped to the whole selected region. These display immediately.

    Addresses are listed here if they are reserved in IPAM. This means either:

    - You [reserved an address via IPAM](/ipam/how-to/reserve-ip/), or
    - You [attached a resource to a Private Network](/vpc/how-to/attach-resources-to-pn/): this triggers IPAM to reserve and attach a private IP address for the resource on the network.

    Addresses which are available but unreserved do not display in the list. For Private Networks, this means the full range of addresses in each [CIDR block](/vpc/concepts/#cidr-block) is not shown.

    <Message type="tip">
    When viewing a private IP address in IPAM, it is shown in [CIDR notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classless_Inter-Domain_Routing#CIDR_notation). For example, the address `172.16.12.2/22` represents the IPv4 address `172.16.12.2` and its associated CIDR block `172.16.12.0/22`, defined on the [Private Network](/vpc/how-to/create-private-network/#how-to-configure-cidr) and containing 1024 addresses.
    </Message>

4. Refine the list using the other filters that display alongside the **Source** filter. Depending on the selected source, you can filter by **VPC**, **Private Network**, **zone**, **IP type** ([IPv4](/ipam/concepts/#ipv4) or [IPv6](/ipam/concepts/#ipv6)), and whether the address is attached to a resource or simply reserved.

    Click **All filters** to display the full set of filters available for the selected source, or **Clear All** to reset them.
