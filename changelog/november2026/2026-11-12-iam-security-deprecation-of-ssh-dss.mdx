---
title: Deprecation of ssh-dss
status: security
date: 2026-11-12
category: security-identity
product: iam
---

SSH keys based on the Digital Signature Algorithm (DSA), also referred to as ssh-dss, are no longer supported. It is not possible to add new SSH keys with the DSA algorithm. Scaleway strongly recommends replacing them with ed25519 or RSA keys. See [How to generate and SSH key pair](/iam/security/create-ssh-key/) for instructions on generating a new one.
