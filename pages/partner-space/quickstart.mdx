The Partner Space is a dedicated section of the Scaleway console allowing partners to manage Scaleway Organizations for their clients.

## Console overview
Discover the Partner Space interface in the Scaleway console.
<GuideFlow src="https://app.guideflow.com/embed/gky99ezs4p"/>

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- Scaleway Partner status

## Get started

1. [Add your first client](/partner-space/how-to/add-client/). 
2. [Edit your client information](/partner-space/how-to/edit-client-information/).
3. [Track your client consumption](/partner-space/how-to/track-client-consumption/).
4. [Manage your customer support tickets](/partner-space/how-to/manage-customer-support-tickets/).
5. Discover the [Partner API](https://www.scaleway.com/en/developers/api/partner-api).
