Resource Explorer is now available in the console. 
This new feature allows you get an overall view and search for all resources from your Organization, with advanced filter options.

Try out the Resource Explorer from your [**Organization dashboard**](https://console.scaleway.com/organization/overview).

See the [Resource Explorer documentation](https://www.scaleway.com/en/docs/account/how-to/use-resource-explorer/) for more information.
