---
title: Private endpoints are now available for Serverless Containers
status: added
date: 2026-10-07
category: serverless
product: containers
---

Serverless Containers can now accept incoming traffic exclusively from your Scaleway private network (ingress). Combined with the already available private egress, this lets you build fully isolated serverless architectures with zero exposure to the public internet.

You have full control over your network exposure:

- Enable or disable the **public default endpoint**
- Enable or disable the **private default endpoint**

For more informations, read [dedicated documentation](/serverless-containers/how-to/secure-a-container/#use-a-private-network-and-a-private-endpoint).
