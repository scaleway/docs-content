status: added
date: 2026-07-31
category: console
product: console
---

The Scaleway console now supports passkey (WebAuthn) authentication. If your passkey supports user verification, it acts as a passwordless login method, combining both authentication factors into a single gesture. Otherwise, it acts as a second factor alongside your password.

Passkeys are currently available for account Owners only, not IAM Members.

Secure your account from the [Account Security](https://console.scaleway.com/settings/account/security) page in your **Settings** space.

See the [Passkey authentication](https://www.scaleway.com/en/docs/account/how-to/use-2fa/#passkey-authentication) documentation for more information.
