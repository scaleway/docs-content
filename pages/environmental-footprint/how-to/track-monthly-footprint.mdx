<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- Environmental data is available only for clients using [supported Scaleway products](/environmental-footprint/additional-content/environmental-footprint-integration/). Data is generated daily and becomes visible the day after product activation.

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Cost & Impact Management** > **Environmental Footprint**. The **Overview** of your Organization environmental footprint appears.
2. Click the **Reports** tab. The Environmental Footprint dashboard appears, with the list of your current and past monthly reports. You can choose between monthly and annual reports by clicking the **Monthly / Yearly** toggle button.
    <Lightbox image={image} alt="A screenshot of the console showing a list of downloadable monthly reports." />
3. Click <Icon name="download" /> next to the report you want to download. The report is downloaded to your local computer in PDF format.
4. Open the PDF file. The following information is available in the document:

    <Lightbox image={image2} alt="" />

    - **1.** The start and end date of the period being tracked.
    - **2.** The name of your Organization.
    - **3.** The total carbon footprint of your Organization over the tracked period. This value includes the impact of the **manufacture** and **use** of all components that run your active Scaleway products, in relation to the useful life of each service.
        <Message type="note">
          For a detailed description of how the carbon emissions are calculated, see the [Environmental Footprint calculation breakdown](/environmental-footprint/additional-content/environmental-footprint-calculator) page.
        </Message>
   - **4.** The total water consumption of your Organization over the tracked period. This value is calculated from the electricity consumption of your services and the [Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE)](/environmental-footprint/concepts/#water-usage-effectiveness) of the data centers that host them.
        <Message type="note">
          For a detailed description of how the water consumption is calculated, see the [Water Consumption section](/environmental-footprint/additional-content/environmental-footprint-calculator/#water-consumption) of the Environmental Footprint calculation breakdown page.
        </Message>
    - **5.** The total water consumption and carbon footprint of each of your Projects.
    - **6.** The total water consumption and carbon footprint per geographical location (region and Availability Zone)
    - **7.** The total water consumption and carbon footprint of each of your products.

Both the carbon footprint and the water consumption are calculated from the power consumption of your active resources. Since power consumption depends directly on how you use your resources, results can vary significantly from one month to the next.

For example, if you start your Elastic Metal server on the 15th of the month and leave it running until the end of the month, the data will be calculated for the 15 days of use, not for the whole month. If you leave the server on, non-stop, in the following month, the impact data will be calculated for a full month.
