## View Organization quotas

1. Click the **Quotas** tab of the [Organization Dashboard](https://console.scaleway.com/organization). The list of quotas filtered by product appears.
2. Click the arrow icon next to the product name to expand or collapse detailed quotas.
3. Click <Icon name="more" /> > **More info** next to the name of your resource of choice. A dialog box appears with the following quota information: Name, Technical name, Product, and Description.

Use the **Filters** section to search your quotas. Modifying one of the following options automatically updates the list of quotas:

1. Search by the precise name of the resource.
2. Specify the product you want to see the quotas for.
3. Specify the location of your resources. This includes regions, Availability Zones, or all locations.
4. Check the box to see used quotas only.
5. Click the **Clear all** button to reset the filters.

## Increase quotas

Quotas are assigned by default according to each account level.

There are two ways you can increase your account level:
    - By [validating your payment method](/billing/how-to/add-payment-method/#how-to-add-a-credit-card)
    - By [verifying your identity](/account/how-to/verify-identity/)

For a detailed list of available quotas and the account levels necessary to increase quota capacity, as well as availability per location, see [Scaleway account - Organization quotas](/organizations-and-projects/additional-content/organization-quotas/).

<Message type="note">
  If you have already validated your payment method and identity and want to further increase your quota, you must [contact Scaleway Support](https://console.scaleway.com/support/create).
</Message>

## Add an Organization

If an existing Organization adds you as a [Member](/iam/concepts/#member), you can add the organization to your list as follows:

1. In the header menu, click the **Organization name** to display the list of Organizations.
2. Click **Add Organization** at the top of the list. The **Log in as an IAM Member** page opens.
3. [Log in as an IAM Member](/iam/how-to/log-in-as-a-member/). The Organization is added to your Organization list.

## Remove an Organization

You can remove Organizations from the list at any time. This does not impact your permissions — logging back into a removed Organization makes it appear in the list again.

1. In the header menu, click the **Organization name** to display the list of Organizations.
2. Click the **Manage Organization list** button at the bottom of the dropdown. The **Manage Organization list** dialog box appears.
3. Select one or several Organizations to remove. You cannot select the current Organization.
4. Click the **Remove from list** button. The page refreshes, and the removed Organizations no longer display.
