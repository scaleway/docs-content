You can now easily manage your Audit Trail logs in your Splunk infrastructure thanks to the Scaleway Add-on for Splunk and the Scaleway App for Splunk. You will find both of them in [Splunkbase](https://splunkbase.splunk.com/apps?page=1&keyword=scaleway). Read [our documentation](/audit-trail/reference-content/splunk-integration/) to learn how to integrate them.
