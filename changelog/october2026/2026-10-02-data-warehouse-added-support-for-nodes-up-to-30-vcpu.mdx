---
title: Support for nodes up to 30 vCPU - 120 GB RAM
status: added
date: 2026-10-02
category: data-and-analytics
product: data-warehouse
---

You can now select up to 30 vCPU (120 GB RAM) as the autoscaling maximum limit for your deployment. Refer to the [documentation](/data-warehouse/how-to/edit-autoscaling/) to learn how to edit the autoscaling parameters for your deployment.
