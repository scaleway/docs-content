As part of the integration of BookMyName into the Scaleway ecosystem, all BookMyName domain names are automatically migrated to Scaleway starting from September 2026. The migration occurs in waves, with each customer receiving email notifications about their specific migration date.

The migration is transparent: your domain names stay online with no downtime, your DNS configuration remains the same, and your domain registration details are preserved. After the migration, you can only manage your domains from the Scaleway console.

This page explains how to access your domains after the migration, and what changes for you.

<Message type="important">
    - After your migration date, the BookMyName interface is no longer available to manage your domain names. You must log in to the [Scaleway console](https://account.scaleway.com/login).
    - Domain names are migrated in waves. You receive email notifications before, during, and after your migration.
</Message>

<Requirements />

- A BookMyName account with active domain names
- The email address associated with your BookMyName account
- Login credentials communicated via email during the migration process

## Access your domains after migration from BookMyName to Scaleway

1. Go to the [Scaleway console login page](https://account.scaleway.com/login).
2. Log in using the credentials communicated in your migration email.
3. Click **Domains & DNS** in the **Domains & Web Hosting** section of the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu. The list of your domain names appears.
4. Click the domain name you want to manage. The **Domain overview** displays, where you can manage your DNS configuration, contacts, renewal settings, and domain lock status.

## About the migration

### Migration timeline

Migrations occur in successive waves starting from September 2026. Each customer receives multiple email notifications:

- An announcement email with your scheduled migration date
- A reminder email before the migration
- A confirmation email after the migration is complete

The migration date is specific to each customer and is indicated in the announcement email.

### What changes

Your domain names are managed from the Scaleway console instead of the BookMyName interface. Your domains run on the Scaleway infrastructure, giving you access to modern management tools and APIs.

### What stays the same

Your domain names remain active throughout the migration process with no downtime. Your DNS configuration, contacts, and registration details are preserved during the migration.

External DNS servers (NS) are not affected by the migration. If you use third-party DNS hosting, your configuration remains unchanged.

Prices and renewal terms remain identical to your BookMyName contract.

### Billing and plan management

Billing for domain renewals is handled through Scaleway. You can manage your payment methods and view invoices from the Billing section of the Scaleway console.

For invoices prior to migration, contact [Scaleway Support](https://www.scaleway.com/en/support/).

### Support

After the migration, Scaleway handles all support for your migrated domains. The BookMyName support ticket history is not accessible after migration. Contact [Scaleway Support](https://www.scaleway.com/en/support/) for any assistance.

### Reseller accounts

Reseller accounts are migrated according to a specific schedule. Resellers receive separate communications about their migration timeline.
The reseller SOAP API is maintained for backward compatibility until further notice.

## FAQ

### Why did I not receive the migration email?

This means your account is not yet scheduled for migration. Wait for the email indicating your migration date. No action is required on your part.

### Why are my domains not appearing after migration?

If you received the confirmation email, but your domains do not appear in the Scaleway console, contact [Scaleway Support](https://www.scaleway.com/en/support/).

### What if I have multiple BookMyName accounts?

Each account is migrated separately. If you have multiple BookMyName accounts, you receive separate migration communications for each.

### What if I already have a Scaleway account?

If your Scaleway account uses the same email address as your BookMyName account, your domains are migrated to that existing account. If the email addresses differ, your domains are migrated to a new Scaleway account, and you can use IAM to manage access across organizations.

### Can I refuse the migration?

The migration is mandatory for all BookMyName domain names.

### Can I change my migration date?

Migration dates cannot be changed. You can perform an outbound transfer before the migration if you do not wish to wait for the scheduled date. See [Transfer a domain name](/domains-and-dns/how-to/transfer-domain-name/) for more information. 

### What if my domain expires during migration?

Contact [Scaleway Support](https://www.scaleway.com/en/support/) if your domain is to expire between September and December 2026.

### What happens to my subdomains and redirects?

For DNS hosted by BookMyName, subdomains and redirects are migrated with your domain. After the migration, they are managed from the Scaleway console like other DNS settings.

If your DNS is not hosted by BookMyName, your subdomains and redirects are not migrated.

### What happens to my email addresses?

Mailbox domains and email accounts hosted by BookMyName are migrated with your domain to [Scaleway Mailbox](/mailbox/). Customers with email accounts are scheduled for later migration waves. More information will be provided in future communications.

### How can I manage access to my domains for multiple users?

Use [IAM](/iam/concepts/#iam) to define custom access rights for each user in your organization.
