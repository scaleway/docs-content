Scaleway charges an authorization amount of €1 and automatically reimburses it within 2 days. A [3D Secure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-D_Secure) validation starts. Depending on your bank, you may need to enter a code sent by SMS or confirm the payment via an app on your smartphone.<br/>
Virtual prepaid cards are not accepted as a payment method.
