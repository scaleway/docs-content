This page provides information about the capabilities and limits of Scaleway Secret Manager, depending on user quotas.

[Organizations](/organizations-and-projects/concepts/glossary/#organization) have quotas, which are limits on the number of Scaleway resources they can use. The following quotas apply to Secret Manager:

| Name                                   | [Payment method validated](/billing/how-to/add-payment-method/#how-to-add-a-credit-card) | Payment method and [identity validated](/account/setup-lifecycle/verify-identity/) |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Secrets                                | 100                                                                                                                                         | 250                                                                                               |
| Versions per secret                    | 20                                                                                                                                          | 20                                                                                                |

<Message type="note">
 To increase your quotas beyond the default limits, contact [Scaleway Support](https://console.scaleway.com/support/create).
</Message>
