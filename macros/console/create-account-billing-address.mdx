1. Enter your company name or registration number and click **Search**, then select your company from the list of results.<br/>
   Alternatively, click **Fill in manually** to enter your details yourself.
    <Message type="note">
      Company lookup is currently available for French customers only. If your company is not found, or if you are not based in France, fill in your details manually.
    </Message>
2. Check and complete your billing address (street address, postal code, city, region, and country).
3. Check and complete your invoicing details (legal company name, SIRET or SIREN number, and VAT number).
4. Click **Next**.<br/>
   You can click **Add later** to [enter your billing information at a later time](/billing/how-to/add-payment-method/), and directly access your Organization dashboard in the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com/organization). Ordering Scaleway resources requires a valid credit card.

    <Message type="important">
      Starting September 1, 2026, French businesses must meet new e-invoicing requirements. Make sure your legal company name, SIRET or SIREN number, VAT number, and billing address are complete and accurate. See the [billing FAQ](/billing/faq/#what-billing-information-do-i-need-to-update-to-comply-with-the-french-e-invoicing-reform) for details.
    </Message>
