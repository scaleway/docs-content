Email forwarding lets you automatically forward emails received in your Mailbox account to another email address, named a destination.

 This feature is useful to:
  - **Centralize your emails**: receive emails from several accounts in a single inbox.
  - **Use a different email client**: read your emails in the client of your choice.
  - **Back up your emails**: keep a copy of each email in another account.

<Message type="note">
  In the current beta version of Mailbox, an email account is limited to 5 destinations.
</Message>

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged into the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- [Configured your Mailbox domain](/mailbox/how-to/configure-mailbox/) with email accounts

## Add a destination to a Mailbox email account

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click the domain you wish to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. Click **Add destination**.
6. Enter the email address to use as a forwarding destination.
7. Click **Add destination**.

## Enable or disable email forwarding

You can enable and disable email forwarding for all destinations set for a Mailbox email account.

<Message type="note">
  If you want to disable email forwarding for a specific destination, you must delete the destination.
</Message>


1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click the domain you want to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. In the **Email forwarding** section, click the **Forwarding** toggle to enable or disable email forwarding for all destinations.
6. (Optional) Click the **Keep a copy in this mailbox** toggle to keep a copy of forwarded emails in the Mailbox email account. This option is disabled by default when email forwarding is enabled.

## Edit a destination

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click a Mailbox domain to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. Click <Icon name="more" /> next to the destination you want to edit and select **Edit destination** from the dropdown. A dialog box appears.
6. Enter the new email address to use as a forwarding destination.
7. Click **Confirm**.

## Delete a destination

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click a Mailbox domain to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. Click <Icon name="more" /> next to the destination you want to delete and select **Remove destination** from the dropdown. A confirmation dialog box appears.
6. Click **Confirm**.
