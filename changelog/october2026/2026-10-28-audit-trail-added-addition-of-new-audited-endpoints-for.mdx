Several new endpoints are now generating events in Audit Trail for the Account Product. Mainly creation, modification and deletion of projects, but also all the authentication endpoints are now generating events. See [our documentation](https://www.scaleway.com/en/docs/audit-trail/reference-content/adt-supported-endpoints/#account) for the list of available events.
