[Object Storage private access](/vpc/how-to/enable-object-storage-private-access/) makes the Object Storage VPC endpoint, `s3-vpc.<region>.scw.eu`, reachable from the Private Networks you select. It adds a private path to your buckets: it does not remove their public endpoint, and does not change their permissions. Most issues come from a client that still targets the public endpoint, or from a bucket policy that does not match the requests coming from the VPC.

## My traffic still transits over the public internet

### Cause

The client keeps targeting the public endpoint of the bucket, `<bucket_name>.s3.<region>.scw.cloud`. Both endpoints stay available when the feature is enabled, and the feature does not redirect the requests sent to the public endpoint.

### Solution

Configure your client with the VPC endpoint, `<bucket_name>.s3-vpc.<region>.scw.eu`. For the AWS CLI, set `endpoint_url = https://s3-vpc.<region>.scw.eu` in your `~/.aws/config` file, as described in [How to reach a bucket over the VPC endpoint](/vpc/how-to/enable-object-storage-private-access/#how-to-reach-a-bucket-over-the-vpc-endpoint).

## My bucket is still reachable from the internet

### Cause

Object Storage private access is enabled on the VPC, but no bucket policy restricts access to the bucket. Enabling the feature adds a private path, and does not remove public access.

Alternatively, a statement of the bucket policy still allows the request. A statement with an `aws:SourceVpc` condition does not make a bucket private on its own: any other statement that allows the request grants access.

### Solution

Apply a bucket policy with an `aws:SourceVpc` condition on the bucket, as described in [How to restrict bucket access to a VPC](/vpc/how-to/enable-object-storage-private-access/#how-to-restrict-bucket-access-to-a-vpc), and check that no other statement of the policy allows requests coming from outside the VPC.

## Resources in my VPC cannot reach my bucket

### Cause

The VPC endpoint is not reachable from the resource, or the bucket policy never matches the requests coming from the VPC.

### Solution

Check the following points:

- Object Storage private access is enabled on the VPC.
- The Private Network of the resource is authorized on the VPC.
- The bucket is in the region of the VPC.
- The client uses the VPC endpoint of the region the bucket is in. A VPC endpoint of another region is not reachable.
- The bucket policy, if any, references the ID of a VPC on which Object Storage private access is enabled. A condition that references any other VPC never matches a request.

## The Scaleway console no longer displays my objects, metrics, or logs

### Cause

The Scaleway console accesses buckets over the internet. When the bucket policy denies requests that do not come from the VPC, the console requests are denied.

### Solution

Manage the bucket from a resource inside the VPC. If you no longer need the restriction, delete or edit the bucket policy.

## I lost access to my bucket

### Cause

The bucket policy allows requests coming from the VPC only, and you have no management method from inside the VPC.

### Solution

The [Owner](/iam/concepts/#owner) of the Organization always has the right to put and delete bucket policies, even when the policy does not allow them to perform other bucket operations. Ask the Owner of the Organization to edit or delete the bucket policy.
