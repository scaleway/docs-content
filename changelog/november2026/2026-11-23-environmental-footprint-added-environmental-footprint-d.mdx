Your environmental impact metrics (Carbon emissions and water consumption) are now available in Scaleway Cockpit, complete with a preconfigured Grafana dashboard.

- Access a ready-to-use dashboard featuring daily, monthly, and yearly comparisons, historical impact trends, and a breakdown of your Top 5 most impactful SKUs for both carbon and water. 
- Query raw carbon and water metrics by Project and SKU directly in Cockpit.
- Correlate environmental data with your infrastructure performance metrics in custom Grafana boards, and set up threshold alerts to keep your footprint in check.

Open Cockpit from the Scaleway console and select the Environmental Footprint dashboard to get started.
