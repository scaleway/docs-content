Forwarding — or email forwarding — lets you automatically redirect every email received by a Mailbox email account to one or more email addresses, named destinations. The email is redirected by the server as-is, with its original sender and content. Forwarding helps you:
  - **Receive your emails in your personal inbox** – Forward the emails of your Mailbox email account to your personal email address, for example a Gmail or Outlook.com address, to follow everything from a single inbox.
  - **Route emails to the people who handle them** – Forward emails sent to a functional address to the colleagues or external partners in charge. For example, you can set emails sent to `invoices@` to be forwarded to your accountants.
  - **Ensure continuity** – Redirect the emails of an employee who cannot access their mailbox or who has left the company to another email address, for example a manager.
  - **Feed your business tools** – Forward incoming emails to a ticketing system, CRM, or archiving solution that receives emails at a dedicated address.

Scaleway does not recommend to use forwarding to:
- Read your Mailbox emails in an external email client – Instead, connect your client to your Mailbox email account using IMAP and SMTP. See [Access your Mailbox from another email client](/tutorials/access-mailbox-from-an-external-client/) for more information.
- Receive the emails of another address of your domain in this Mailbox – Instead, use an [alias](/mailbox/how-to/add-alias/).

<Message type="note">
  Forwarding is limited to 5 destinations per Mailbox email account.
</Message>

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- [Configured your Mailbox domain](/mailbox/how-to/configure-mailbox/) with email accounts

## Add a destination to a Mailbox email account

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click the domain you wish to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. Click **Add destination**.
6. In the **Destination address** field, enter the email address to use as a forwarding destination.
7. Click **Add**.

## Enable or disable email forwarding

<Message type="note">
  When forwarding is enabled, incoming emails are not kept in your Mailbox email account. Check **Keep a copy in this mailbox** if you want to forward and also receive incoming emails.
  
  Disabling forwarding resets the mailbox to receive and retain incoming emails as default.
</Message>

You can enable and disable email forwarding for all destinations set for a Mailbox email account. Note that if you want to disable email forwarding for a specific destination, you must delete the destination.

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click the domain you want to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. In the **Email forwarding** section, click the **Forwarding** toggle to enable or disable email forwarding for all destinations.
6. (Optional) Check **Keep a copy in this mailbox** to keep a copy of forwarded emails in the Mailbox email account.

## Edit a destination

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click a Mailbox domain to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. Click <Icon name="more" /> next to the destination you want to edit and select **Edit destination** from the dropdown. A dialog box appears.
6. In the **Destination address** field, enter the new email address to use as a forwarding destination.
7. Click **Confirm**.

## Remove a destination

<Message type="note">
  Removing all destinations disables forwarding. Incoming emails will be received and retained as default.
</Message>

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Mailbox**. The list of your domains appears.
2. Click a Mailbox domain to configure. The list of Mailboxes created for this domain appears.
3. Click a Mailbox email account to configure. The Mailbox account information appears.
4. Navigate to the **Forwarding** tab.
5. Click <Icon name="more" /> next to the destination you want to delete and select **Remove destination** from the dropdown. A confirmation dialog box appears.
6. Click **Remove**.
