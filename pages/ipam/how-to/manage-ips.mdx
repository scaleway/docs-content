IPAM acts as a single source of truth for the IP addresses of your Scaleway resources. You can use IPAM in the Scaleway console to manage any address held in your Organization, whatever its source:

- [Private IP addresses](/ipam/concepts/#private-ip-address) on your Private Networks
- Public zonal IP addresses, scoped to a single Availability Zone such as `fr-par-1`
- Public regional IP addresses, scoped to a whole region such as `fr-par`

Addresses are managed here whether you [reserved them with IPAM](/ipam/how-to/reserve-ip/) or they were attached to one of your resources.

The management actions currently available include:
- Viewing the details of an IP address, including its ID, source, tags, creation date and to which resource (if any) it is attached
- Editing the tags of an IP address

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged into the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- At least one [reserved IP address](/ipam/how-to/reserve-ip/), or at least one resource [attached to a Private Network](/vpc/how-to/attach-resources-to-pn/)

## View the details of an IP address

1. Click **IPAM** in the **Network** section of the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu.

2. Select the region of the IP address you want to manage, using the drop-down menu at the top of the page.

3. Select the **source** of the address: **Private IPs**, **Public zonal IPs**, or **Public regional IPs**. Use the other filters that display alongside it to narrow the list down to the required address, as described in [how to list your IP addresses](/ipam/how-to/list-ips/).

4. Click the <Icon name="more" /> icon next to the IP address whose details you want to view, then click **More info**.

    A dialog box appears.

    You can view the following information:
    - The IP address
    - The **source** of the IP address, and its scope: the Private Network for a private IP, the Availability Zone for a public zonal IP, or the region for a public regional IP
    - Which **resource** (if any) it is attached to
    - The **ID** of the IP address
    - The date on which the IP was **created**. This corresponds to the date it was reserved in IPAM, either through a manual reservation or when it was attached to a resource.
    - The date on which the IP was last **updated**
    - The **tags** associated with the IP address.

5. Click **OK** to exit the screen.

## Edit the tags of an IP address

Tags help you organize your IPAM IP addresses. You can assign as many tags as you want to each address, whatever its source, and use them for identification and sorting purposes.

1. Follow steps 1 to 4 [above](#view-the-details-of-an-ip-address).

2. Click into the **Tags** box, and type the tag(s) you want to add. Alternatively, click the **x** symbol to remove any existing tags.

3. Click **OK** to exit the screen.
