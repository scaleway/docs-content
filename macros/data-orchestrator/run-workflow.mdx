---
macro: run-workflow
---

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Data & Analytics** > **Data Orchestrator**. The list of your workflows appears.
2. Click the name of the workflow you want to run. The workflow **Overview** tab opens.
3. Click the **Actions** button, then click **Run** in the drop-down menu. Your workflow starts, and a new entry appears in the **Runs** tab.

<Message type="tip">
    On the **Data Orchestrator** page, you can also click the run icon <Icon name="play" /> next to the workflow you want to run.
</Message>
