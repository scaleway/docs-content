Commitment plan for Elastic Metal is a flexible pricing option that allows you to choose between a fully flexible pay-as-you-go pricing or a commitment period for Elastic Metal servers. By committing to a plan, you can enjoy better rates than pay-as-you-go pricing.

The following billing options are available:
  - **No commitment** (Hourly): Billed based on the exact number of hours your server is allocated during the month. Ideal for short-term workloads or testing.
  - **Monthly plan**: Offers discounted monthly pricing. A one-time setup fee applies upon creation.
  - **Long-term commitment** (12, 24, or 36 months): Provides the highest discount rates. Setup fees are waived for all long-term commitments.

You can, at any moment, upgrade to a longer commitment plan. It is not possible, however, to downgrade to a lower duration, or from a commitment plan to hourly billing. In such cases, you must delete your Elastic Metal server and create a new one.

<Requirements />
- A Scaleway account logged into the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- An [Elastic Metal server](/elastic-metal/how-to/create-server/)

## Upgrade your commitment plan

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Bare Metal** > **Elastic Metal**. The Elastic Metal dashboard opens.
2. Click the name of the server you want to configure. The server information page opens.
3. Scroll down to the **Commitment plan** section, where your current plan is displayed. Click **Upgrade plan**. A dialog box appears.
4. Select the plan you want to upgrade to and click **Review** to move to the next step.
5. Review the server and options details and the new pricing. Note that switching to monthly commitment incurs a one-time setup fee, which is charged immediately.
    <Message type="important">
        Reverting to hourly billing or downgrading to a lower commitment plan will not be possible.
    </Message>
5. Check the box to accept the new conditions and set up fee (if any).
6. Confirm the new billing period by clicking **Update billing**. The switch is effective after a few minutes (during which the server is in a `migrating` state).

## Manage auto-renewal

You can activate or deactivate auto-renewal for long-term commitment plans (12, 24, or 36 months). Without auto-renewal, the server reverts to a standard monthly commitment plan when reaching the end of the commitment period.

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to **Bare Metal** > **Elastic Metal**. The Elastic Metal dashboard opens.
2. Click the name of the server you want to configure. The server information page opens.
3. Scroll down to the **Commitment plan** section:
    1. Click the **Enable auto-renewal** button to activate renewal. This button is only available if your server and plan is eligible for renewal.
    2. Click the **Enable auto-renewal** button to deactivate renewal. This button is only available for long-term plans with automatic renewal already activated.
