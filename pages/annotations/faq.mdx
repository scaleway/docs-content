## Overview

### What is Annotations?

Scaleway Annotations lets you attach custom key‑value metadata to Scaleway resources. Using Annotations, you can organize and structure your instances, buckets, databases, etc., for use in FinOps, GreenOps, governance, and other initiatives.

### Why do I need Annotations?

Annotations gives you a lightweight, flexible way to add structured metadata to Scaleway resources, making it easier to search, manage, audit, and optimize your resources.  

Common use cases for Annotations include:

- **Navigation and filtering** – Annotate resources so you can filter and search by environment, team, cost‑center, or any custom attribute.  
- **Governance and access control** – Combine annotations with IAM or policy bindings to restrict actions on resources that carry certain annotations (e.g., only the `security` team can modify resources marked `sensitive=true`). 
- **FinOps: Cost tracking and reporting** – Use annotations for precise budgeting reports, tracking cloud spend across teams, projects, or clients.
- **GreenOps: Environmental tracking** - Pair annotations with [Environmental Footprint](/environmental-footprint/) to attribute and optimize the carbon and water footprint of your workloads.
- **Workflow automation** – Trigger scripts, policies, or scaling actions based on specific annotation values (e.g., automatically back up all resources with the annotation `environment=prod`).


### What is the difference between tags and annotations?

**Tags** are free-form labels that you can attach to individual resources to help organize, sort, filter, and monitor them. Scaleway products (e.g., [Instances](/instances/concepts/#tags), [Elastic Metal](/elastic-metal/concepts/#tags)) manage their own tags. They are not shared across products or across the Organization as a whole.

**Annotations** are structured key-value metadata that you can attach to a Scaleway resource. An annotation consists of a **key** (e.g., `Team`, `Environment`, `Cost-center`) and a **value** (e.g., `Marketing`, `Production`, `83546`), which are stored in a centralized dictionary. Once created, keys and values can be bound to your resources using their [Scaleway Resource Names (SRNs)](/annotations/reference-content/scaleway-resource-names/). This centralized, structured approach makes annotations suitable for FinOps, GreenOps, and governance use cases such as cost allocation, reporting, auditing, and automation, and it comes with dedicated [IAM permission sets](/annotations/setup-lifecycle/configure-annotations-permissions/) to control who can manage keys, values, and bindings.

In short:

- **Tags** are quick, informal labels scoped to a single product, ideal for simple sorting and filtering.
- **Annotations** are formal, governed, Organization-wide key-value metadata, ideal for cross-product organization, cost management, and governance.
