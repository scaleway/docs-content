This page shows you how to change the [offer type](https://www.scaleway.com/en/pricing/network/#public-gateway) of your Public Gateway. You can upgrade to a superior offer type to take advantage of higher bandwidth, or downgrade to an inferior offer type to reduce costs.

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- [Created a Public Gateway](/public-gateways/how-to/create-a-public-gateway/)

1. In the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com) side menu, go to the **Network** section and click **Public Gateways**. A listing of your Public Gateways displays.
2. Use the **region selector** at the top of the page to filter for the region of the Public Gateway you want to modify, then click the Public Gateway in the listing. The Public Gateway's **Overview** page opens.
3. Click the **Settings** tab, go to the **Offer** panel, then click **Change offer**.  A dialog box appears. Alternatively, you can also click **Change offer** next to the Public Gateway type on the **Overview** page, or select **Change offer** from the **Quick Actions** menu.
4. Select the new offer type from the **Public Gateway type** drop-down list. Each offer type displays its bandwidth and hourly price.
    <Message type="note">
    Some offer types, such as `VPC-GW-XL`, may require a quota increase. If so, click **Request quotas** next to the offer type to submit a request.
    </Message>
5. Click **Change offer** to confirm.
    <Message type="important">
    Your Public Gateway is unavailable for a few seconds while the offer changes. During the operation, its status temporarily changes to `failed`, then returns to `ready`. Your existing IP address is preserved.
    </Message>

Your Public Gateway now uses the new offer type, which displays under its name and in the **Type** field of its **Overview** page.
