When you order a product that integrates the Environmental Footprint Calculator, you can see the estimated impact of the product before you order.

<Message type="important">
The footprint calculator is available on select products and will be extended to all products. See the [Environmental Footprint calculator product integration](/environmental-footprint/concepts/environmental-footprint-integration/) to view supported products and upcoming rollouts.
</Message>

This page uses Elastic Metal servers as an example to explain the estimation, which is based on the calculation methodology detailed in the [Environmental Footprint calculation breakdown](/environmental-footprint/concepts/environmental-footprint-calculator/) page.

## Availability Zone footprint estimation

When you select your Elastic Metal server Availability Zone, you can see the quantity of CO₂ equivalent (CO₂e) emitted per kWh consumed for each zone.

<Lightbox image={image} alt="screenshot of the Scaleway console showing the different availability zones in which you can create an Elastic Metal server. For each AZ, three green leaves indicate the level of environmental impact – the system is explained below." />

This data is determined by the [Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)](/environmental-footprint/concepts/glossary/#pue-power-usage-effectiveness) of the data center and the [energy mix](/environmental-footprint/concepts/glossary/#energy-mix) of the country in which the data center is located.

<Message type="note">
  Scaleway uses [Ember's](https://ember-climate.org/) Electricity Data Explorer to determine the impact of the energy mix. The energy mix can be very different from one country to another depending on the used source of electricity. The [Electricity Maps' Environmental Impact by Zone map](https://app.electricitymaps.com/map) shows these differences.
</Message>

The estimation displays up to 3 green leaf icons depending on the estimated consumption of the AZ. The higher the number of leaves, the lower the quantity of CO₂e emitted per kWh consumed, and the lower your impact.

Hovering your cursor over the AZ to select displays the environmental footprint estimation in gCO₂e/kWh.

<Message type="important">
  The AZ footprint estimation considers CO₂ emissions per kWh of electricity consumed, based on the energy mix and Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of the data center. This value is AZ-specific; for example, in PAR-2, each kWh consumed may have an impact of 0.065 kgCO₂e.

  Meanwhile, the offer footprint estimation is a more complex calculation that combines the AZ-specific data with additional factors relevant to the specific offer. These factors are outlined in the [Environmental Footprint calculation breakdown](/environmental-footprint/concepts/environmental-footprint-calculator/) page.
</Message>

## Elastic Metal offers footprint estimation

The Elastic Metal offers environmental footprint estimation considers the impact of the **manufacturing** and **use** of all the elements that allow the service to work. This includes the network, cross-IT equipment and stock, and non-IT elements in addition to the manufacturing and use of the server itself. Your choice of AZ impacts the **use** by multiplying each kW consumed by each of these elements by the value indicated.

<Message type="note">
  See the [Environmental Footprint calculator breakdown](/environmental-footprint/concepts/environmental-footprint-calculator/) page to learn more about the calculation of the Scaleway services environmental impact.
</Message>

To estimate the electricity consumption usage, Scaleway tested each offer—using different compute configuration—to ensure the coverage of a large variety of power consumption set-ups. 

While Scaleway simulated various use cases to have an average power consumption, the actual consumption still depends on how you use your server.

Scaleway estimates the electricity consumption of the server, and then calculate its estimated impact based on the energy mix of the selected zone and the PUE of the data center.

The estimation displays up to 3 green leaf icons depending on the estimated consumption of the offer. The higher the number of leaves, the lower the environmental impact.

<Lightbox image={image2} alt="screenshot of the offer selection section of the Elastic Metal order page in the Scaleway console showing the different offers available to order. For each offer, three green leaves indicate the level of environmental impact - the system is explained below." />

Hovering your cursor over the environmental footprint indicator displays the environmental footprint estimation in gCO₂e per hour or per month, depending on your billing method.

The **Environmental footprint details** section of your order summarizes the impact estimation, including an indication to help you imagine what this impact represents, such as `≈ 35 km by car`.
