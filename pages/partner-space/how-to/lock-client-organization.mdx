You can lock a client's Organization if they do not respect the Scaleway Terms of Service.

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- Scaleway Partner status
- [Added a client](/partner-space/how-to/add-client/)

<Message type="important">
  Locking an Organization prevents its owner from ordering or managing resources.
</Message>

1. Go to the Partner Space. A list of your clients appears.
2. Locate the row corresponding to the client whose Organization you want to lock.
3. Click <Icon name="more" /> at the end of the row.
4. Click **Lock Organization**. A dialog box appears.
5. Type **LOCK** in the text area.
6. Click **Lock** to confirm.
