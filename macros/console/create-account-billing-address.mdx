At any point during this step, you can click **Switch to Personal project** at the bottom of the page to continue creating your account without entering all your company details. You can [switch from a personal account to a corporate account](/account/setup-lifecycle/switch-account-to-corporate/) after creation.

1. Enter your company name or registration number and click **Search**, then select your company from the list of results.<br/>
   If your company is located outside France or does not appear in the search, click **Fill in manually** to enter your details manually.
2. Check and complete your billing address (street address, postal code, city, region, and country).
3. Check and complete your invoicing details (legal company name, SIRET or SIREN number, and VAT number).
4. Click **Next**.<br/>
   You can click **Add later** to [add a payment method](/billing/how-to/add-payment-method/) at a later time, and directly access your [Organization dashboard](https://console.scaleway.com/organization) in the Scaleway console. Ordering Scaleway resources requires a valid credit card.

<Message type="important">
  Starting September 1, 2026, French businesses must meet new e-invoicing requirements. Make sure your legal company name, SIRET or SIREN number, VAT number, and billing address are complete and accurate. See the [billing FAQ](/billing/faq/#what-billing-information-do-i-need-to-update-to-comply-with-the-french-einvoicing-reform) for details.
</Message>
