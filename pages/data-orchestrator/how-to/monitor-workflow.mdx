<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged into the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- Created a [Data Orchestrator workflow](/data-orchestrator/how-to/create-workflow/)

## Monitor a running workflow

1. Click **Data Orchestrator** in the **Data & Analytics** section of the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu. The list of your workflows appears.
2. Click the name of the workflow you want to monitor. The workflow **Overview** tab appears.
3. Make sure the last run status is `Running` or `Queued`.
4. Click **View run** next to the last run status. The run information appears.

## Monitor previous runs

1. Click **Data Orchestrator** in the **Data & Analytics** section of the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu. The list of your workflows appears.
2. Click the **Runs** tab. The previous runs of all workflows appears.
3. To focus on the runs of a specific workflow, click the name of the workflow you want to monitor.
4. Click the eye icon corresponding to the run you want to monitor. The run information appears.

## Delete previous runs

1. Click **Data Orchestrator** in the **Data & Analytics** section of the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu. The list of your workflows appears.
2. Click the **Runs** tab. The previous runs of all workflows appears.
3. To focus on the runs of a specific workflow, click the name of the workflow you want to monitor.
4. Click the <Icon name="delete"/> icon corresponding to the run you want to delete.
    If needed, use the checkboxes on the left to select multiple runs.
5. Type **DELETE** and click **Delete workflow run(s)** to confirm.
