## Add your organization details

1. Select an organization type (Corporate, Startup, Consultant, Association, or Personal project).
2. Select your country.
3. For Corporate, Startup, Consultant, and Association organization types, select your company industry.
4. Enter a name for your Scaleway Organization.
5. Click **Next**.

## Add your phone number

<Message type="note">
  This step is not mandatory unless you intend to use Dedibox.
</Message>

1. Enter your phone number and click **Send code**.
2. Enter the 6-digit code you received on your phone number.
3. Click **Verify phone number**.

## Add your billing address

1. Enter your company name or registration number and click **Search**, then select your company from the list of results.<br/>
   Alternatively, click **Fill in manually** to enter your details yourself.
    <Message type="note">
      Company lookup is currently available for French customers only. If your company is not found, or if you are not based in France, fill in your details manually.
    </Message>
2. Check and complete your billing address (street address, postal code, city, region, and country).
3. Check and complete your invoicing details (legal company name, SIRET or SIREN number, and VAT number).
4. Click **Next**.<br/>
   You can click **Add later** to [enter your billing information at a later time](/billing/how-to/add-payment-method/), and directly access your Organization dashboard in the [Scaleway console](https://console.scaleway.com/organization). Ordering Scaleway resources requires a valid credit card.

    <Message type="important">
      Starting 1 September 2026, French businesses must meet new e-invoicing requirements. Make sure your legal company name, SIRET or SIREN number, VAT number, and billing address are complete and accurate. See the [billing FAQ](/billing/faq/#what-billing-information-do-i-need-to-update-to-comply-with-the-french-e-invoicing-reform) for details.
    </Message>

## Add your payment method

Scaleway charges an authorization amount of €1 and automatically reimburses it within two days. A [3D Secure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-D_Secure) validation starts. Depending on your bank, you may need to enter a code sent by SMS or confirm the payment via an app on your smartphone.<br/>
Virtual prepaid cards are not accepted as a payment method.
