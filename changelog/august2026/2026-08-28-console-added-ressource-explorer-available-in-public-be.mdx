Resource Explorer is now available in the console. 
This new feature gives you a complete view of all resources in your Organization, and allows you to search them using advanced filters.

Try out the Resource Explorer from your [**Organization dashboard**](https://console.scaleway.com/organization/overview).

See the [Resource Explorer documentation](/account/how-to/use-resource-explorer/) for more information.
