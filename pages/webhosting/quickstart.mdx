Web Hosting is a service that allows individuals and organizations to make their website accessible on the internet. It involves storing website files and data on a server, which is connected to the internet and enables users to access the website through a web browser.

Scaleway provides several [Web Hosting plans](https://www.scaleway.com/en/web-hosting/) for individuals, professionals, and everyone in between.

All Scaleway Web Hosting plans use a user-friendly Web Hosting control panel tool.
This interface provides users with intuitive graphical navigation and quick access icons for configuring and monitoring their hosting solutions. With features like website publishing, database launching, email creation, and file management, users can efficiently manage their hosting tasks through this interface, ensuring elevated performance.
Scaleway provides Web Hosting plans with [cPanel](/webhosting/reference-content/cpanel-reference-content/) and WordPress. Check out our documentation on [Understanding cPanel](/webhosting/reference-content/understanding-differences-cpanel-plesk/).

<Requirements />

- A Scaleway account logged in to the [console](https://console.scaleway.com)
- [Owner](/iam/concepts/glossary/#owner) status or [IAM permissions](/iam/concepts/glossary/#permission) allowing you to perform actions in the intended Organization
- [Registered a domain name](/domains-and-dns/how-to/register-internal-domain/) at Scaleway or another registrar

## How to order a Web Hosting plan

### Web Hosting configuration

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Web Hosting**.
2. Click **Order Web Hosting**. The Web Hosting configuration screen opens.
3. Select the region in which to create your Web Hosting.
4. Choose your commitment and hosting plan.
5. Configure options:
     - Enable backup: for daily automated backups of your website, emails, and databases. Retains backups for 3 days, with a full weekly backup retained for 2 months.
     - Dedicated IP: for improving email reputation and SSL setup.
6. Choose a management interface for the content and settings of your website:

    - cPanel is a web-based control panel for managing web hosting accounts, emails, and websites through a graphical interface.

    - WordPress is a user-friendly content management system (CMS) that lets you create, edit, and manage your website without needing to code or technical skills.
        <Message type="important">
         If you choose WordPress as your management interface:

         - You will receive a direct access link to your email box
         - Each WordPress hosting plan supports **only one website**
         - You cannot create additional databases beyond the one that is automatically provided
         
         Find out more about what the WordPress plan includes in the [Web Hosting FAQ](/webhosting/faq/)
        </Message>
7. Enter your email address. Scaleway will send your management interface password to this email address.
8. Click **Configure domain**.

## How to configure DNS for Web Hosting

To link your domain name with your Web Hosting plan you have to update the nameservers in the DNS configuration at your registrar. Nameservers are servers used to translate domain names into IP addresses. When you enter a URL in a browser, they show you where the website is located, instead of memorizing entire numerical sequences in the form of IP addresses.

<Message type="important">
  If you are migrating from another web hosting provider to Scaleway Web Hosting, you might need to [update your nameserver information](/webhosting/how-to/configure-dns-webhosting/) with the information available in your console.
</Message>

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Web Hosting**. The **Web Hosting** overview page opens.
2. Click <Icon name="more" /> or the domain name of the Web Hosting you want to configure. The **Hosting information** page opens.
3. Click the **DNS verification** tab. The DNS configuration page opens.
4. Update your DNS servers. Two options are available:
    - Use Scaleway DNS servers.
        We provide DNS servers for your Web Hosting plan that contain all required DNS configuration. To use these nameservers, update the nameservers associated with your domain name with the servers provided by Scaleway.
    - Manual DNS update (advanced).
        If you prefer to continue using your current DNS servers, update the DNS zone at your registrar with all DNS records provided.
    <Message type="note">
        It can take 24 to 48 hours until your DNS updates are propagated on the internet.
    </Message>
5. Click **Verify domain** after you have updated your DNS configuration to launch the DNS verification process. When the process is complete, a green check with the text **Configured** appears next to the nameservers and records to configure.

## How to access your hosting dashboard from the Scaleway console

Your hosting dashboard is the admin interface where you can manage the content and settings of your website.

<Message type="tip">
    You can manage your [email accounts](/webhosting/how-to/manage-email-accounts/), [databases](/webhosting/how-to/manage-databases/), and [FTP accounts](/webhosting/how-to/manage-ftp-accounts/) directly from the Scaleway console.
</Message>

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Web Hosting**. The **Web Hosting** overview page opens.
2. Click <Icon name="more" /> and **More info**, or the domain name of the Web Hosting plan you want to configure. The **Overview** tab displays information about your Web Hosting, such as your credentials to access your hosting dashboard.
3. In the **Hosting information** section, click **Open hosting dashboard**. The cPanel or WordPress hosting dashboard opens in a new tab.

Retrieve your username under **Main user name** in the **Hosting information** section and your password in the **Initial password** section.


## How to create a mailbox from the Scaleway console

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Web Hosting**. The **Web Hosting** overview page opens.
2. Click <Icon name="more" /> and **More info**, or the domain name of the Web Hosting service you want to configure. The **Hosting information** page opens.
3. Click the **Emails** tab to open information related to your email accounts.
4. Click **Create email account** to create a new one. A dialog box opens.
5. Select the domain you want to associate the email account with from the dropdown, then enter a username and password.
    
    The username is the part of your email address before the `@`.
6. Click **Create email account** to confirm the action and create the account.

To access webmail for the email account, click **Access webmail** next to the email account.

## How to access the webmail interface from the Scaleway console

You can access the webmail platform for your Web Hosting directly from your Scaleway console.

1. In the [console](https://console.scaleway.com/) side menu, go to **Domains & Web Hosting** > **Web Hosting**. The **Web Hosting** overview page opens.
2. Click <Icon name="more" /> and **More info**, or the domain name of the Web Hosting service you want to configure. The **Hosting information** page opens.
3. Click the **Emails** tab to display information related to your email accounts.
4. Click **Access webmail** next to the email address you want to access.

The webmail interface opens in a new browser tab.
