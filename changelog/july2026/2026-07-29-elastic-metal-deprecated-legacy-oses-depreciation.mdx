Effective October 1st, support for several operating systems will be officially deprecated due to reaching their End of Life (EOL). 

Below is the list of affected OS versions along with their respective EOL dates:

- Ubuntu 20.04: EOL May 2025
- Debian 10: EOL June 2024
- Debian 11: EOL August 2026
- Proxmox 7: EOL July 2024
- Proxmox 8: EOL August 2026
- CentOS 7: EOL June 2024
- Windows Server 2019: EOL September 2024

We recommend upgrading your infrastructure to supported OS versions ahead of the deadline to ensure continued security and system stability.
