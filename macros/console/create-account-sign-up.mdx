1. Click **Sign Up** in the upper right corner of the page. The account creation page opens.
2. Select a sign-up method, and follow the corresponding steps.
    <Tabs>
      <TabsTab label="Sign-up with SSO">
        1. Select the service you want to use (Google, Microsoft, or GitHub). Make sure the associated email address is the one you want to use for your Scaleway account.
        2. Check the box to agree to the Scaleway [Terms and Services](https://www.scaleway.com/en/contracts/), and Scaleway [Data Protection Agreement](https://www.scaleway.com/en/contracts/).
        3. Click **Confirm email address** to continue.
      </TabsTab>
      <TabsTab label="Sign-up with your email address">
        1. Click **Continue with your email**.
        2. Enter your email address, first name, and last name.
        3. Check the box to agree to the Scaleway [Terms and Services](https://www.scaleway.com/en/contracts/), and Scaleway [Data Protection Agreement](https://www.scaleway.com/en/contracts/).
        4. Click **Confirm email address** to continue.
        5. Click the validation link sent to your email address.
      </TabsTab>
    </Tabs>
