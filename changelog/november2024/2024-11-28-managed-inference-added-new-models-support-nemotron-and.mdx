[Llama 3.1 Nemotron 70B](/generative-apis/reference-content/supported-models/#llama-31-nemotron-70b-instruct) and [Molmo 72B](/generative-apis/reference-content/supported-models/#molmo-72b-0924) are available for deployment on Managed Inference.

Nemotron improves human-like responses in complex tasks, while Molmo provides increased accuracy on multimodal inputs (text and images).
