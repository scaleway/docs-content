[Private Networks](/serverless-containers/concepts/#private-networks) let your Serverless Containers communicate in an isolated and secure network without needing to be connected to the public internet. Each container can be connected to one or several Private Networks, letting you build your own network topologies.

For full information about Scaleway Private Networks and VPC, see the [VPC documentation](/vpc/) and its [best practices guide](/vpc/reference-content/getting-most-private-networks/).

## Attach a Serverless Container to a Private Network

<Message type="tip">
  A container attached to a Private Network can use a private endpoint to communicate with other resources attached to the same Private Network.
</Message>

<Tabs>
  <TabsTab label="Existing container">
  1. Click **Containers** in the **Serverless** section of the side menu. The containers page appears.
  2. Click the relevant containers namespace.
  3. Click the name of the container you want to manage.
  4. In the **Network & Domains** tab, click **Attach Private Network** and select a Private Network from the dropdown.
  5. Enable or disable the private endpoint for your container. Enabling the private endpoint lets your container communicate with other resources attached to the same Private Network.
  6. Enable or disable the public endpoint for your container. Disabling the public endpoint prevents your container from being accessed from the public internet.
  7. Click **Save container settings**.

  Your container is redeployed, and is now attached to the selected Private Network.
</TabsTab>
<TabsTab label="New container">
  1. Click **Containers** in the **Serverless** section of the side menu. The containers page appears.
  2. Click the **containers namespace** in which you want to create your container.
  3. Click **Create container**. The container creation wizard appears.
  4. In the **Network & Domains** tab, click **Attach Private Network** and select a Private Network from the dropdown.
  5. Enable or disable the private endpoint for your container. Enabling the private endpoint lets your container communicate with other resources attached to the same Private Network.
  6. Enable or disable the public endpoint for your container. Disabling the public endpoint lets you isolate your container from the public internet.
  7. Configure the other parameters of your container, then click **Create container**. See [Build and push a container image](/serverless-containers/how-to/build-push-container-image/) for more information.

  Your new container is deployed, and is attached to the selected Private Network.
  </TabsTab>
</Tabs>

## Detach a container from a Private Network

<Message type="warning">
  Detaching a container from a Private Network disables its private endpoint and may end connectivity with other resources attached to the same Private Network.
</Message>

1. Click **Containers** in the **Serverless** section of the side menu. The containers page appears.
2. Click the relevant containers namespace.
3. Click the name of the container you want to manage.
4. In the **Network & Domains** tab, under **Private Networks and endpoint**, click **Detach Private Network**.
5. **[TO BE CONFIRMED with console]** Type **Confirm** in the pop-up that asks you to confirm the action, then click **Detach Private Network**.
6. Click **Save container settings**.

Your container is redeployed and is now detached from the Private Network.

## Delete a Private Network

<Message type="note">
  Before deleting a Private Network, you must [detach](/vpc/how-to/attach-resources-to-pn/#how-to-detach-a-resource-from-a-private-network) all resources attached to it.
</Message>

This is done from the VPC section of the console. See [Delete a Private Network](/vpc/how-to/delete-private-network/).
