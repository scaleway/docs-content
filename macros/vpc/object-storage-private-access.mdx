---
macro: object-storage-private-access
---

Object Storage private access allows resources in your Private Networks to reach Object Storage over the internal Scaleway network, instead of the public internet.

Buckets are always served on two endpoints, whether the feature is enabled or not:

| Endpoint     | Regional URL                 | Bucket URL                                  |
|--------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Public       | `s3.<region>.scw.cloud`      | `<bucket_name>.s3.<region>.scw.cloud`       |
| VPC          | `s3-vpc.<region>.scw.eu`     | `<bucket_name>.s3-vpc.<region>.scw.eu`      |

Enabling Object Storage private access on a VPC makes the VPC endpoint reachable from the Private Networks you select, for all buckets in the region of the VPC. Without it, the VPC endpoint is not reachable from a Private Network.

The feature adds a private path to your buckets. It does not remove the public path, and it does not change the permissions of your buckets.
