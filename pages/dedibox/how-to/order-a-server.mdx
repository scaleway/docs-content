[Scaleway Dedibox](https://www.scaleway.com/en/dedibox/) are high-quality dedicated servers. With over 15 years of experience, Scaleway provides dedicated servers which are recognized for their extreme reliability and best-in-class redundancy.

<Message type="tip">
  You can also order Dedibox servers using the [Scaleway console](/dedibox-scaleway/how-to/order-dedibox/), to benefit from the whole Scaleway ecosystem and the latest console.
</Message>

<Requirements />

- A Dedibox account logged in to the [console](https://console.online.net)
- Added [a payment method](/dedibox-account/how-to/add-payment-method/) to your account

1. In your [Dedibox console](https://console.online.net) homepage, click **Order** > **Dedicated server**. The server offers page opens. 
    You can view all servers available in your preferred data center by clicking the **Datacenter selection** drop-down menu in the top right corner.
2. Select a server model and click the **Order** button next to your choice. The server creation wizard opens.
3. Complete the following steps in the wizard:
    - Select your commitment period: rent without commitment, or choose a 12-24-36 month plan for lower monthly rates and free server setup.
      <Message type="note">
        This step only appears for Dedibox servers eligible to a commitment plan.
      </Message>
    - Select the data center. Some server models are available in more than one data center. If you have no preference, select **"No preference"**.
    - Choose the service level. There are two plans available:
      - **Basic service level** - Free plan included with any Dedibox server:
        - Ticket support
        - Phone support
        - Sales support
        - SLA (See the [Service Level Agreement for Bare Metal Services](https://www.scaleway.com/en/bare-metal/sla/) for more details)
        - RPN (Real Private Network) with limited bandwidth
      - **Business service level** - Paid plan adapted to the professional use of a Dedibox server:
        - Ticket support
        - Phone support
        - Sales support
        - Priority technical support
        - SLA (See the [Service Level Agreement for Bare Metal Services](https://www.scaleway.com/en/bare-metal/sla/) for more details)
        - RPN (Real Private Network) with high bandwidth
        - Customization (disks, bandwidth)
        - Security options (DDoS)
    - Configure the available options:
        <Message type="note">
          The list may vary according to your Dedibox server and your service level plan.
        </Message> 
      - Select a backup option: 750 GB FTP, 100 GB FTP, or **no FTP backup**
      - Choose a plan for protection against DDoS
      - Select an extra network-attached storage, or **No SAN**
      - Select the public bandwidth
4. Click **Order**. A summary of your order appears.
5. Select your payment method in the **Payment mode choice** drop-down menu in the top right corner.
6. Check the boxes confirming that you have read and agreed to the terms of service.
7. Click **Confirm my order**. A confirmation of your order appears.
