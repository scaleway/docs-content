  ## Data Orchestrator

  ### Data Orchestrator supported endpoints

    The following [Data Orchestrator](/data-orchestrator/) endpoints are supported and will be logged in Audit Trail:

    - CreateWorkflowDefinition
    - UpdateWorkflowDefinition
    - DeleteWorkflowDefinition
    - UpdateWorkflowVersion
    - StartWorkflowDefinition
    - TerminateWorkflowRun
    - PauseWorkflowRun
    - ResumeWorkflowRun
    - DeleteWorkflowRun
