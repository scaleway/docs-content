A snapshot takes a picture of a specific volume at one point in time. For example, you may have a server with one volume containing the OS and another containing the application data, and you want to use different snapshot strategies on both volumes. [Creating snapshots of your volumes](/block-storage/how-to/create-a-snapshot/) gives you total freedom of which volumes you want to back up, while [images](#image) are more convenient for full backups of your Instance.

You can create a snapshot directly from a volume, or generate it automatically when you create an Instance image. In both cases, the snapshot exists as an independent, billed resource and is not deleted when its source volume, Instance, or image is deleted.
