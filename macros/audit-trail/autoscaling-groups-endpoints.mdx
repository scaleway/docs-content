  ## Autoscaling Groups

  ### Autoscaling Groups supported endpoints

    The following [Autoscaling Groups](/instances/how-to/create-manage-instance-groups/#autoscaling) endpoints are supported and will be logged in Audit Trail:

    - CreateGroup (`POST/autoscaling/v1alpha2/zones/{zone}/groups`)
    - UpdateGroup (`PATCH/autoscaling/v1alpha2/zones/{zone}/groups/{group_id}`)
    - DeleteGroup (`DELETE/autoscaling/v1alpha2/zones/{zone}/groups/{group_id}`)
</Accordion>
