In accordance with [ICANN](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/about-icann) requirements, Scaleway must verify the email address associated with your domain name. This verification may be required following a domain registration, an incoming transfer, or an update to the registrant's contact information.

You can receive legitimate email verification requests which can sometimes appear in your spam folder. This page explains how to identify legitimate verification emails and complete the verification process.

<Message type="important">
  Complete verification within 15 calendar days. Failure to verify will suspend your domain name and disable dependent services, including websites and email.
</Message>

## Identify legitimate verification emails

### The email is sent from Scaleway, BookMyName or Online

1. Open the verification email you received.

    <Message type="important">
      Before clicking the verification link, ensure it redirects to a legitimate domain. If the destination is different or the message appears suspicious, do not proceed and contact [Scaleway Support](https://console.scaleway.com/support/tickets/create).
      Your login and password information will not be asked during this process.
    </Message>

    If the sender and verification link match the examples in the following table, then the email is legitimate.

    |         Sender          |                      Verification link                         |
    | :---------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
    | @bookmyname.com         | `https://bookmyname.com/waps.cgi?key=XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`         |
    | @registrar.scaleway.com | `https://registrar.scaleway.com/waps.cgi?key=XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX` |
    | @registrar.online.net   | `https://registrar.online.net/waps.cgi?key=XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`   |

2. Click the link to verify your email address.

### The email is sent from a different sender

You can receive legitimate email verification requests from other senders (the email address can be customized by resellers).

1. Open the verification email you received.

    <Message type="important">
      Before clicking the verification link, ensure it redirects to https://emailverification.info/. If the destination is different or the message appears suspicious, do not proceed and contact [Scaleway Support](https://console.scaleway.com/support/tickets/create).
      Your login and password information will not be asked during this process.
    </Message>

2. To verify your email address, click the link to https://emailverification.info/ and enter the verification code.
