You can now easily manage your Audit Trail logs in your Splunk infrastructure thanks to the Scaleway Add-on for Splunk and the Scaleway App for Splunk. You will find both of them in Splunkbase. Read [our documentation](https://www.scaleway.com/en/docs/audit-trail/reference-content/splunk-integration/) to learn how to integrate them.
